Continuity brief: Urgent-care clinics

SupplyLens Network Simulator, services model

With no response, on-time service falls from 100.0% to 92.6%, 3,084 customers give up waiting, and the disruption costs an estimated $240,190 in lost revenue and late penalties; service is back to normal on day 34 (4 days after the disruption ends). The best response is load sharing: it costs $7,778, lifts on-time service to 95.5% and leaves the business an estimated $78,049 better off than doing nothing.

How to read this

This is a scenario, not a forecast. The network, demand, revenue per request, staffing costs and the size and length of the disruption are assumptions. Demand is taken at its expected value each day. Differences between plans are estimates under those assumptions, not measured savings.
